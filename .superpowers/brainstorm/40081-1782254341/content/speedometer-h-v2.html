H — Color Progresses Red → Green

At 0 the ring is fully red. As contacts increase, it shifts through orange/yellow toward green at 30.

5 contacts — Red

15 contacts — Yellow

28 contacts — Green

The ring color interpolates smoothly: Red (0) → Orange (10) → Yellow (15) → Green (30)
Color is calculated dynamically based on contact count — not a gradient on the stroke, but the entire ring shifts color.
